The gap

Most AI work passes through three hands. Value leaks at every handoff.

AI projects rarely fail because of the technology. They stall when nobody owns the next step.

01 · Strategy

The strategy deck

It gets approved in a boardroom. Nobody who wrote it is in the room when the real trade-offs turn up.

handoff
02 · Build

The build team

It builds to the demo. The hard question, what happens when the AI is wrong in front of a customer, gets put off.

handoff
03 · Run

The run team

It inherits a system it didn't design. Tracing is in place. Nobody is checking whether the answers are still right.

01 / 06 · Strategy

Frame the decision worth automating.

The most expensive mistake is a clever answer to the wrong question.

What I own
Picking the one decision where AI earns its keep, the business number it should move, and who signs off when it gets that decision wrong.
Your team keeps
A one-page value thesis that leadership has agreed to, with a clear measure of success.
Evidence
2×A stalled business unit doubled in one year once the plan was rebuilt around what customers pay for, rather than around IT systems.
02 / 06 · Prototype

Prove it with your own experts.

A demo shows that something can work. A test set shows how often it does.

What I own
Building the first working version. I sit with your experts to turn what they know into a test set, run each case repeatedly, and report plainly how often it is right and how it fails.
Your team keeps
An evaluation set it owns, and an honest answer on whether this should be an agent, a fixed workflow or not built at all.
Evidence
10%yearly revenue growth on a national programme, where changing how people worked, not the AI model, delivered the result.
03 / 06 · Production

Ship it to the people who rely on it.

This is the step where most pilots stall. Here it is the point.

What I own
Architecture, data checks, what the system is allowed to read, the records it keeps, what it costs to run, and a phased rollout with a plan for when things go wrong.
Your team keeps
A system in daily use, with the reasoning behind every design choice written down.
Evidence
Zerodowntime across a 50TB migration, delivered in phases on a programme where an outage would have been a regulatory event.
04 / 06 · Operate

Run it with a person who can say "stop".

AI working at machine speed still needs someone who can overrule it.

What I own
Monitoring, clear data ownership, a record of where every number comes from, and a quick, well-known way for a person to step in.
Your team keeps
A runbook, an override path, and the confidence to explain why the system made last Tuesday's decision.
Evidence
8 wks → 0Twelve business areas were waiting up to two months for each data request. Letting each one own its data removed the queue entirely.
05 / 06 · Evaluate live

Learn from real use, not from the demo.

Every override, correction and complaint is a signal. Most systems throw them away.

What I own
Measuring quality on live traffic, logging every decision and every human correction, and feeding them back into the test set.
Your team keeps
A live view of how often the system is right this week, not just at launch.
Evidence
40% → 0Staff were overriding a fraud system four times in ten. It was rebuilt around the decision they actually make, not the model's score.
06 / 06 · Compound

Improve it, then hand over the loop.

Each cycle starts from better evidence than the last. That is what keeps it improving.

What I own
Adjusting the instructions, the data and which model handles what, so the system keeps working when the AI model or vendor underneath it changes. Then I take the next decision back to Frame.
Your team keeps
A loop it runs confidently on its own after I leave, with evidence for a regulator rather than promises.
Evidence
25 yrswithout a failed flagship platform, across banking, healthcare and manufacturing.

How I'm embedded

Forward-deployed, independently.

The AI labs now place forward-deployed engineers inside their customers' teams, because a strong model on its own rarely reaches production. I work the same way, but I'm independent of any vendor.
