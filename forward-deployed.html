Why AI has to be grown

Software is finished at launch. AI is not.

Most AI projects are still run like software projects: specify, build, hand over, move on. That's how an AI system ends up worse a year after launch than it was on launch day.

Conventional software

  • At launchDone. The spec fixes how it behaves.
  • QualityTested once, before release.
  • Over timeStays the same until someone changes it.

An AI solution

  • At launchDay one. The context it's given shapes how it behaves.
  • QualityMeasured all the time, with evals built from real cases.
  • Over timeDrifts as data, users and models change, or improves if someone is growing it.

So the person who sets the strategy can't hand off at go-live. Most of the value arrives after it.
