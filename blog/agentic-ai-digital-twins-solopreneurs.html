Agentic AI: Monetizing Your Own Digital Twin

VRVikram Rathour, enterprise data and AI
Contents

As a follow-up to my earlier post about pseudonymized avatars, I decided to paint an imaginary, uninhibited view of what the world would look like with this new agentic AI phenomenon, and what it really means for mere mortals like us. Is it a blessing? And if yes, what would I look like in that era? The ideas expressed are completely fictitious and I have no idea if reality will indeed be anything like this. However, the idea is to imagine a win-win where humans and machines can coexist. Hope you enjoy reading it.

In the digital era, social media platforms thrive by monetizing user data, turning their users into the product. They generate massive revenue streams by leveraging personal information for targeted ads, content curation, and more. But what if individuals could reclaim control and monetize their own data? Enter agentic AI, a revolutionary concept that enables individuals to harness the power of their own digital twins, transforming them into solopreneurs.

Digital twins and personas

A digital twin is a comprehensive, AI-driven representation of an individual, comprising multiple personas that reflect various facets of life. Here's a breakdown of the key personas:

Interoperability with digital contracts using pseudonymized avatars

One of the key enablers of this ecosystem is interoperability. Digital twins need to seamlessly interact with various external services and platforms. This is achieved through digital contracts and pseudonymization.

  • Standardized interactions: digital contracts ensure consistent, transparent, and secure interactions between your digital twin and external service providers.
  • Cross-platform compatibility: these contracts facilitate seamless integration across different platforms, allowing your digital twin to engage with various ecosystems effortlessly.
  • Pseudonymized avatars: the data shared with service providers is represented through pseudonymized avatars of your real-world personas. This ensures privacy and confidentiality compliance, safeguarding personal information while still enabling valuable insights and interactions.
  • Automated agreements: smart contracts automate the negotiation and execution of agreements, reducing the need for manual intervention and ensuring efficiency.

Multi-agent system and AI currency

The ecosystem operates on a multi-agent system where each persona is managed by a dedicated AI agent. These agents collaborate and negotiate with external service providers, leveraging a blockchain-based AI currency to facilitate transactions.

  • Agent-to-agent negotiation: your digital twin's agents aggregate personalized deals and offers from various provider agents across entertainment, financial services and e-commerce, ensuring the best outcomes for you.
  • AI currency: this decentralized currency ensures transparent, secure, and seamless value exchange, empowering individuals to monetize their data directly.

Leverage the power of edge computing

With the power of modern digital devices, the latest NVIDIA chips and edge computing, all data processing and AI-driven insights can occur locally, at the edge. This provides:

  • Enhanced security: sensitive data remains on personal devices, reducing exposure to cyber threats.
  • Increased privacy: users have greater control over their data, minimizing reliance on centralized servers.
  • Real-time processing: faster, real-time insights and recommendations, tailored to individual needs.

Monetization opportunities for solopreneurs

Democratizing the digital economy

Agentic AI enables individuals to take control of their digital footprint, turning data into a personal asset. This democratization:

  • Empowers data monetization: individuals can now profit from their own data, rather than letting platforms reap the rewards.
  • Fosters economic independence: by becoming solopreneurs, people can diversify income streams and leverage their digital presence.
  • Promotes privacy and control: users decide how, when, and with whom to share their data, maintaining full sovereignty over personal information.

Conclusion

In my imaginary future world, individuals are no longer the product being sold in someone else's marketplace, but the owners of their digital destiny. Agentic AI offers a path to personal empowerment and economic democratization. By creating and monetizing their digital twins, people can reclaim control, transforming their data into a valuable asset.

This shift heralds a new era where everyone has the opportunity to become a solopreneur, leveraging their digital personas for personal profit and meaningful engagement. With edge computing ensuring security and privacy, digital contracts facilitating interoperability, AI currency symbolizing wealth and pseudonymized avatars protecting personal information, the future is not just digital. It is personal, secure, and empowering.

Disclaimer: This post is completely fictional and I have tried my best to create an imaginary picture of how life will be. I am sure there are many mistakes, and at some point I have stretched my imagination a bit too far. But then, I did warn you at the start.
